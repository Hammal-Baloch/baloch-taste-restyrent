About us

Baloch Taste Restaurant is a family-friendly place for people who love honest, full-flavoured food. Our kitchen is built around Balochi cooking, where meat is cooked slowly and seasoned simply.

Alongside our Balochi dishes, we serve Pakistani favourites and fresh BBQ. Everything is cooked to order, so your food reaches the table hot.

What we serve

  • Traditional Balochi dishes
  • Pakistani curries, rice and bread
  • Charcoal BBQ and grills
  • Dine-in and takeaway
